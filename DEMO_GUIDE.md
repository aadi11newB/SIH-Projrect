## 🚀 Live Demo Step-by-Step Actions

### Step 1: Open the Application
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000`.
2. Observe the high-impact defense/research landing page with the animated flowchart:
   `GIS → Climate → Shelter → Solar → Optimization`.
3. Click **"Explore Demo (Leh, Ladakh)"** or **"Start Designing Shelter"**.

### Step 2: Tab 1 — GIS & Location Intelligence
1. The Map opens focused on **Leh, Ladakh** (Latitude: 34.15°N, Longitude: 77.58°E, Elevation: 3,524m MSL).
2. Toggle map layers:
   - **Solar GHI Heatmap** (Displays intense solar radiation gradient in cold arid desert).
   - **Diurnal Temp** (Displays extreme sub-zero swings).
   - **Topography MSL** (Topographic contour contours).
3. Scroll to the **GIS Strategic Site Ranking Matrix** to compare Leh, Pune, Jaisalmer, Dras, and Srinagar.

### Step 3: Tab 2 — Solar Site & PV Analyzer
1. Observe the **Solar Potential Score (94/100)**.
2. Note the calculated **Recommended Tilt (39.1°)** and **True South (180°)** orientation.
3. Review the visual **Roof Zone A Solar Packing Array** showing modules arranged with zero-shadow spacing.
4. Review the **12-Month Harvest Curve** totaling **13,200 kWh/yr**.

### Step 4: Tab 3 — Shelter & 3D Visualizer
1. Click and drag on the interactive **Three.js 3D Shelter Visualizer** to rotate and zoom around the building.
2. Toggle the **PV Array ON/OFF** button to see roof-mounted solar modules.
3. Switch between **Thermal Gradient** and **Realistic** rendering modes.
4. Adjust building length or roof pitch on the parametric controls and see the 3D model update.

### Step 5: Tab 4 — 24-Hour Thermal Simulator & Day/Night Diagnostics
1. Examine the **24-Hour Diurnal Temperature Chart**:
   - Outdoor ambient (blue dashed line) drops to **-18.5°C**.
   - Indoor temperature (teal solid line) is maintained comfortably at **+12.8°C to +23.4°C** without any diesel heating.
2. Hover over any hour to see the real-time instantaneous **Solar Heat Gain (W)** vs **Envelope Conduction Loss (W)**.
3. Review the **Day/Night Thermal Dynamics Monitor** and the **IS 3792 Thermal Comfort Index (TCI: 92/100)**.

### Step 6: Tab 5 — Elite Solar + Thermal Co-Optimization
1. Highlight the **"Best Overall Configuration"** card.
2. Explain the engineering trade-off:
   - *Design A*: High solar, poor thermal.
   - *Design B*: Extreme insulation, low solar export.
   - *Design C (Co-Optimized)*: Optimal balance of 100mm PUF, 10cm Rockwool, 8.0 kW PV, and ₹5.35L net cost.
3. Click **"Load Configuration"** to apply it across the entire platform.

### Step 7: Tab 6 — Real-Time “What If?” Simulator
1. Move the **Wall Insulation Thickness slider** from 2 cm to 15 cm:
   - Watch indoor minimum temperature instantly lift from -4°C up to +16°C.
2. Rotate the **Orientation slider** away from South:
   - Watch the thermal score drop as solar aperture capture decreases.

### Step 8: Tab 7 — Government Subsidies & Financial Planner
1. Review the itemized **Bill of Materials (BOM)** breakdown.
2. Point out the verified matched schemes:
   - **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana**: ₹78,000 CFA.
   - **LREDA Cold Desert Defense Subsidy**: Up to ₹3,50,000 capital support.
3. Note the **Simple Payback Period (2.9 Years)** and **10-Year Cumulative Savings (+₹13.8 Lakhs)** vs diesel generators.

### Step 9: Tab 9 & PDF Report Generation
1. View the **Final Recommended Configuration Card**.
2. Click **"Download Official PDF Report"** (or top navbar button).
3. The backend compiles and downloads `GeoTherm_Shield_Report_leh_ladakh.pdf`.
4. Open the PDF to showcase the publication-quality technical dossier with tables, charts, and statutory disclaimers!
